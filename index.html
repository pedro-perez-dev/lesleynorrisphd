About Dr. Norris

Dr. Norris is a licensed clinical psychologist who helps children, teens, and adults take back control from anxiety and OCD using evidence-based treatments like exposure-based cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), exposure and response prevention (ERP), and CBT for insomnia (CBT-I). Her approach is collaborative, practical, and individualized, with the goal of helping anxiety and OCD take up less space in the lives of her clients and their families.

Dr. Norris completed her graduate training at Temple University under Dr. Philip C. Kendall, where she specialized in evidence-based treatment for childhood anxiety, including the Coping Cat program. She completed her residency and postdoctoral fellowship at the Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University, where she later served as an Assistant Professor (Research) and as a staff psychologist in the Intensive Program for OCD and Anxiety-Related Disorders at the Pediatric Anxiety Research Center.

Dr. Norris has authored more than 35 peer-reviewed publications, many focused on improving and expanding evidence-based treatments for anxiety and OCD. She brings this research expertise into her clinical practice, translating the latest science into practical, effective strategies that clients can use in everyday life. Whether working with a child, teenager, or adult, she strives to create a supportive environment where clients can build the skills and confidence to move toward the things that matter most to them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you located?

My practice is based in Rhode Island, and I can see clients in any PSYPACT member state.

Do you accept insurance?

I am credentialed with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Rhode Island. If you do not have BCBSRI, but you do have out-of-network benefits, you may be able to get reimbursed for therapy sessions. You will receive a superbill (a detailed statement of services) that you can submit to your insurance company to seek reimbursement.

How do I know if my insurance will cover therapy?

It is a good idea to check with your insurance provider to understand your coverage. Some helpful questions to ask:

  • Do I have out-of-network behavioral health benefits?
  • What is my deductible?
  • What percentage of the psychologist’s fees will the insurance company reimburse for outpatient psychotherapy?
  • Are there limits on the number of behavioral health sessions?
  • What is the allowed amount for CPT 90791 (intake), 90837 (individual therapy), and 90846 (family therapy sessions)?

What is your cancellation policy?

You will be charged your regular session fee for missed appointments or appointments that are canceled with less than 24 hours notice.

What are your fees?

Please contact me directly to discuss current session fees. I am committed to making therapy accessible and offer a limited number of sliding scale slots.

Do you offer in-person appointments?

All my sessions are virtual and take place via a secure, HIPAA compliant telehealth platform.

How do I get started?

Schedule a free 15-minute phone consultation to get a sense of whether my approach might be a good fit for you or your family. Email me at contact@lesleynorrisphd.com to find a time that works for you.
